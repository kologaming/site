# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 26, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the **Monster Tower Balancer iOS App** ("the App").

## Information We Collect

We, as the app developer, do **not directly collect**, store, or share any personal information or data from users. This includes:

- No name, email address, or contact information
- No account or login
- No personally identifiable information on our servers

However, please note:
- **Third-party ad networks** may collect certain data when ads are shown (see Advertising section below)
- **Analytics and crash reporting** may be collected anonymously by Google Firebase (see Analytics section below)
- **Apple services** (iCloud, Game Center, In-App Purchases) are provided by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy

## Advertising

The App shows **interstitial ads between rounds** and offers **optional rewarded video ads** (for example, to double coins or get a second chance). Ads are provided by **Google AdMob**.

### Third-Party Ad Networks

Google AdMob and its partners may collect certain information to serve and measure advertisements. This may include:

- Device information (device type, operating system)
- Advertising identifier (IDFA) — **only if you allow tracking** in the App Tracking Transparency prompt
- Ad interaction data (views, clicks)
- Approximate location (based on IP address)

If you choose **"Ask App Not to Track"**, ads are still shown but are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

You can remove interstitial ads with the **Remove Ads** in-app purchase.

We do not control the data collection practices of these third parties. Please review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information.

## Analytics

The App may use **Google Firebase** to collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (such as app version, device model and in-app events) so we can improve the game. This data is not used to identify you personally.

## Apple Services

- **iCloud:** your game progress (coins, unlocked monsters, settings) may be synced between your devices through your own iCloud account. We do not have access to this data.
- **Game Center:** leaderboards and achievements are handled by Apple Game Center if you are signed in.
- **In-App Purchases:** all payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

## Offline Gameplay

The App is fully functional **offline**. An internet connection is required only to show ads, sync with iCloud and use Game Center.

## Age Restrictions

The App is rated **4+** and is suitable for all ages.

## Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is designed to be safe for all ages, including children.

Please note that third-party ad networks may collect certain data as described in the Advertising section above. Parents and guardians should be aware that the App contains advertisements and in-app purchases, and may want to supervise their children's use of the App.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
