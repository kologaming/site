# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 17, 2026_

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the **iVerb** app ("the App") for iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS, developed by Maksym Kolotii (Bundle ID `com.mkolotii.iVerb`).

In short: **iVerb does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** It works fully offline and contains no analytics, ads, or trackers.

## Information We Collect

We do **not collect** any personal data. This includes:

- No account, sign-up, or login
- No personal information requested or stored
- No analytics or usage tracking (no Firebase, no third-party SDKs)
- No advertising and no ad identifiers (no IDFA, no App Tracking Transparency)
- No crash or diagnostics reporting

The App makes **no network requests**. The entire verb list ships inside the App bundle and is used entirely offline.

## Local Data

The only data the App stores is your **Favorites** — the words you star are saved locally on your device using SwiftData. This data:

- Is stored only on that device
- Is never synced to a server (no iCloud/CloudKit sync is enabled)
- Never leaves your device

No other user data is stored.

## No Tracking, No Analytics, No Ads

The App contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking technologies of any kind. No third-party SDKs or services are integrated.

## System Features Used

The App uses certain on-device system features. None of these transmit data off the device or to the developer:

- **Text-to-speech** (AVSpeechSynthesizer): pronounces a word on-device. No microphone, no recording, no data sent. No permission required.
- **Spotlight indexing** (Core Spotlight): adds the static verb list to your device's local Spotlight index so verbs can be found in iOS search. This is a local OS index; nothing is transmitted.
- **Handoff / Continuity** (NSUserActivity): passes only the selected verb's identifier between your own Apple devices via Apple's Handoff. No data goes to the developer.
- **In-app purchase** ("Say Thanks" tip, StoreKit 2): the optional tip is processed entirely by Apple. The App never sees or stores payment or financial information.

## Permissions

The App requests **no permissions**. It shows no permission prompts (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, notifications, health, etc.).

## Third-Party Services

There are **no third-party SDKs, services, analytics, or ad networks**. The only third party involved is **Apple**, solely for the optional in-app purchase, which is governed by [Apple's own privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children. No data is knowingly collected from users under 13, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## Data Security

Because the App collects no data and makes no network requests, there is no transmission of your information to secure. The only stored data (Favorites) never leaves your device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
