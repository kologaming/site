## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: I encountered a bug. What should I do?
A: Please email us with:
- Your device model (e.g., iPhone 14)
- iOS / iPadOS / watchOS version
- A short description of the issue
- Screenshots or screen recordings (if possible)

---

### Q: Do you collect any personal information?
A: No. iVerb collects no data at all, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, ads, or trackers. You can read our [Privacy Policy](https://gms.kolotii.com/privacy-policy/iverb/privacy-policy) for full details.

---

### Q: Where are my favorites stored?
A: Your starred words are saved locally on your device only. They are never synced to a server and never leave your device. Deleting the App removes this local data.

---

### Q: Do I need an internet connection to use the App?
A: No. iVerb is fully offline — the entire verb list ships inside the App.

---

### Q: Can I sync my favorites across devices?
A: Favorites are stored locally on each device and are not synced through a server. Handoff lets you pass the currently selected verb between your own Apple devices, but starred favorites are not synced.

---

### Q: What is the "Say Thanks" tip?
A: It's an optional in-app purchase to support development. It is processed entirely by Apple; the App never sees or stores any payment information.
